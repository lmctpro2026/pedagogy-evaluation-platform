How it works

Answer. Score. Reflect.

Every response is weighted and read against the Firmin (2020) techno-pedagogical practice framework.

  1. Step 1 About 5 minutes

    Answer

    Rate 20 statements from Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree. Answers save on your device as you go, and you review them all before submitting.

  2. Step 2 Instant

    Score

    See a score out of 100 for each of the four dimensions, plus an overall profile of your techno-pedagogical practice.

  3. Step 3 PDF · APA cited

    Reflect

    Read a written report with interpretations and targeted recommendations, export it as a PDF, and — with an account — compare against past years.

The framework
"Effective teaching with technology is not the substitution of practice, but the intentional alignment of pedagogy and tools toward defined learning outcomes." — Firmin (2020), Monash University

Dr Selena Firmin's doctoral research examined how IT academics perceive and apply teaching philosophy, identifying four interrelated dimensions of techno-pedagogical practice. This platform turns that framework into a practical, repeatable self-assessment.

  • TP Teaching Practice Student-centred design, reflective adaptation. Statements 1–5
  • PD Pedagogical Development Research engagement, collegial growth. Statements 6–10
  • TA Technology Adoption Confident, evaluated use of digital tools. Statements 11–15
  • TPP Techno-Pedagogical Practice Intentional integration of pedagogy and technology. Statements 16–20
Your data

What we keep, and for how long.

What's stored
Your 20 answers, the four category scores, and any optional demographics you choose to give. Anonymous attempts are stored only once you submit, without a name.
How long
Three years, then deleted automatically. With an account, one result is kept per calendar year — a retake replaces that year's result.
Who can see it
You, and the research team led by Dr Selena Firmin at Federation University Australia. Nothing is shared with third parties.
