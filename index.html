The framework

“Effective teaching with technology is not the substitution of practice, but the intentional alignment of pedagogy and tools toward defined learning outcomes.”

— Firmin (2020), Monash University

Dr Selena Firmin's doctoral research examined how IT academics perceive and apply teaching philosophy, identifying four interrelated dimensions of techno-pedagogical practice. This platform turns that framework into a practical, repeatable self-assessment.

  • TP Teaching Practice Student-centred design, reflective adaptation. Statements 1–5
  • PD Pedagogical Development Research engagement, collegial growth. Statements 6–10
  • TA Technology Adoption Confident, evaluated use of digital tools. Statements 11–15
  • TPP Techno-Pedagogical Practice Intentional integration of pedagogy and technology. Statements 16–20

Your data

What we keep, and for how long.

What's stored
Your 20 answers, the four category scores, and any optional demographics you choose to give — stored only when you submit within the 10-minute session. Anonymous attempts are stored without a name.
How long
Three years, then deleted automatically. With an account, up to two attempts a year are kept: your newest (active) result and the previous one, which you can delete at any time.
Who can see it
You, and the research team led by Dr Selena Firmin at Federation University Australia. Nothing is shared with third parties.
