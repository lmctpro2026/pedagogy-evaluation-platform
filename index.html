PDF · APA cited
"Effective teaching with technology is not the substitution of practice, but the intentional alignment of pedagogy and tools toward defined learning outcomes." — Firmin (2020), Monash University · ProQuest D&T Global

Dr Selena Firmin's doctoral research examined how IT academics perceive and apply teaching philosophy, identifying four interrelated dimensions of techno-pedagogical practice. This platform operationalises that framework into a practical, repeatable self-assessment.

The Four Dimensions

Each dimension captures a distinct aspect of how educators integrate technology into their pedagogical repertoire. Your score across all four dimensions produces a holistic profile of your techno-pedagogical practice.

TP Teaching Practice Student-centred design, reflective adaptation. →
PD Pedagogical Development Research engagement, collegial growth. →
